Real-observation contract and prospective empirical pilot, 2026-10-25

Primary source: https://icecube.umd.edu/PublicData/
Additional release inventory: https://icecube.wisc.edu/science/data-releases/
KM3NeT source: https://github.com/KM3NeT/KM3-230213A-data

Observation audit
-----------------
The downloaded IceCube ODF files contain calibrated reconstructed pulse charge,
time and position in triggered/filtered records. These are not raw ADC waveforms
or certified independently detected unit photons. Almost all charge values are
fractional PE. The original ODF files and unchanged HDF5 values remain preserved.
The ODF float decoder uses the upstream decimal-mantissa representation; it was
checked against the published first IC59 event and source-tool decoder.

Native identity matching was rejected after the audit: the supplied geometry
matches IC59, but 2013 reported positions differ by as much as 23.423 m. Nearest
identities are ambiguous for several reported positions. The aborted strict
import is retained in data/neutrino/pulse_study/train.h5; it was not trained.

The separately versioned v2 pilot uses fixed Voronoi spatial cells anchored on
the supplied 5,480-position geometry, with a predeclared 50 m maximum quantization
distance. It permits explicit many-to-one cell assignments; it does not claim
cross-run DOM identity. Raw pulse coordinates and fractional charges are saved
exactly alongside cell indices. No test-pulse-derived recentering is performed.

Many 2013 pulse timestamps exceed the reported event duration, including ms
tails. Use a globally fixed 0--64 us analysis window on unmodified source time,
not a guessed event-length exposure mask or A+B-dependent event alignment.
The analysis crop is a qualified preprocessing choice made after schema/clock
audit, before any real-model performance was examined. All source pulses remain
in storage; outside-window counts are reported. In validation, 8,225 of 15,975
pulses lie outside this window. This is a substantial limitation.

No actual per-run live-sensor status, complete DAQ exposure/dead-time record or
zero-interaction labels accompany these samples. The array mask represents
candidate spatial support, not certified live sensors. It cannot support an
absolute physical rate, efficiency correction or physical no-event classifier.

Predictive objective
--------------------
Each split unit is one whole reconstructed pulse with its charge mark. Assign
the unchanged PE amplitude to fixed categories [0,.5), [.5,1), [1,2), [2,infinity).
This is categorical mark prediction, never rounding charge into photon counts.
Keep whole pulses/marks together when constructing A/B. The loss is the raw
held-out pulse count-weighted joint cell/time/charge-category log score. Report
the arrival marginal by summing over mark categories as a separate metric.

This is an empirical conditional prediction risk on response-processed pulses.
It does not invoke independent-Poisson thinning theory or claim latent photon
intensity recovery. Known analytic photon GT is not real-data GT.

Prospective split and budget: config/neutrino_pulses.yml
------------------------------------------------------
Train: run 115150 / 2010 IC59, 8,348 records, 466,724 pulses inside analysis window,
       124 empty records and 5,266 records with 1--30 pulses.
Validation: run 121585 / 2013 enriched selection, 71 records, 7,750 in-window
            pulses, one empty, no records with 1--30 in-window pulses.
Test: run 122252 / 2013 large-NChannel selection, 1,329 records; this is a bright,
      differently selected population, not an independent low-count real test.

Runs never cross folds. Overlapping reported acquisition intervals within a run
are grouped for descriptive within-run uncertainty. One run per fold cannot
quantify new-run generalization uncertainty or all possible time dependence.

One seed, three epochs, batch two, unchanged declared optimizer and strict
time/memory caps. Dense temporal convolutions share parameters across cells;
four mark channels are internal, avoiding a DOM-pair matrix. Full FP32 is used
because development studies exposed BF16 logit drift on the separate simulation.
Choose checkpoint and finite mixture parameters by all-marked-pulse validation
NLL. Low-count checkpoint selection is not supported by this real validation
sample. Do not pretend that the bright real test establishes a low-count claim.

Report frozen test scores once, including failure to beat the simple estimator,
clock/window loss, charge-mark contract, position quantization, memory/time and
domain shifts. No clean-real-photon GT or physics gain is inferred.

No-event/zero-bias evidence
--------------------------
The ODF source explicitly describes triggered/filtered events. Its empty records
do not establish no physical interaction. The available single-event KM3NeT and
Glashow releases likewise do not supply an unbiased, exposure-labelled no-event
population for this pilot. L1/coincidence-selected timeslices require their own
trigger/exposure/calibration audit and are not automatically zero-bias windows.

The public release inventories were checked again; no downloaded sample here
provides the needed complete zero-bias/live/exposure contract. Retain that claim
as unsupported. A future no-event study requires documented untriggered/zero-bias
windows and detector-state information, or an explicitly different empirical
background task. No request was sent to a collaboration or external contact.
