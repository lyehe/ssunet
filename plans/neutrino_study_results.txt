Completed phased study: photon self-splitting and dense prediction
2026-10-25

The planned engineering, independent simulation test, finite mechanism/response
studies, empirical real-pulse pilot and separate labelled probe were executed.
Positive and negative results are retained. This is evidence for a restricted
predictive claim, not proof of physical Poisson detection, clean intensity
recovery, no-event classification, general neutrino reconstruction benefit or
novelty of photon splitting itself.

Independent main comparison
---------------------------
New cohort: 79,771 training / 10,181 validation / 10,048 reserved test events.
The test contains 10,028 stored configuration families. All known relatives of
the pilot were excluded; no known family crosses folds. Unknown deeper latent
relations and the archived source-generation/selection contract remain qualified.

Prediction is a relative probability over fixed sensor/time support, conditional
on A, p and geometry/support. No MC labels, held-out counts or clean rate maps
enter the forward pass. The training loss keeps raw count weighting. PSNR is not
an acceptance metric, and a random held-out photon realization is not clean GT.

Three seeds use unchanged 3,585-parameter backbones, ten epochs and identical
frozen validation/test photon splits at p=.5. Checkpoints are chosen by the
registered low-count validation rule. Baseline selection evaluated 36 prior and
144 mixture candidates on development data only. The selected low-count control
uses 25 ns input smoothing, no spatial smoothing and a 25% selected-prior mixture.

The first sealed main test passed both quality contrasts, the 0.10 nats/photon
practical margin, and the resource limits. Scores are mean per-event seed NLLs,
not a probability ensemble, and seeds are not counted as extra test events.

Primary model-minus-selected-control delta: -0.245084 nats/photon, descriptive
95% paired group interval [-0.272125,-0.218388]. Model-minus-donor delta:
-4.710704 [-4.822572,-4.597531]. Primary donor matching coverage is 100%.
Unmatched bright-stratum donor contrasts are not interpreted as exact controls.

The primary stratum has 5,135 events, 3,662 with nonempty B, and 12,261 held-out
photons. All-event scoring uses 62,745 held-out photons in 6,059 events. Empty
targets stay in the catalogue and contribute zero location NLL. Intervals use
10,000 whole-configuration bootstrap draws and do not include model/selection
uncertainty. The frozen mixture still leads overall, limiting any general claim.

Engineering throughput was 684 examples/sec with a 1.68 GiB process peak and
0.078 GiB allocated GPU peak. Completed main runs stayed below 4 GiB process,
12 GiB allocated GPU, 1,800 training sec/seed and 600 total evaluation sec/seed.
The main test cost 222--227 sec/seed. Measurements distinguish allocated tensor
memory from other processes and CUDA context/driver memory.

Numerical instability and precision diagnostic
---------------------------------------------
Main seed 43 degraded from low-count validation NLL 4.141 at epoch 2 to 223.771
at epoch 10. All-event final NLL was 191.032; fresh training loss also diverged.
The registered validation selection kept epoch 2. This failure is visible in
the complete curves and all final-budget artifacts, rather than hidden by selection.

A short matched FP32/BF16 study on the separate small cohort found almost no
difference: low-count test delta +0.000829 [-0.000535,+0.002210]. Both precisions
were stable at 6,000 updates; this does not reproduce the later failure regime.

A separately declared diagnostic therefore forked the failing main seed at
epoch 2, preserving weights, optimizer, RNG, exact cursor and prefix counters,
then continued through epoch 5 in FP32. At identical total updates (24,930), its
fresh-family test NLL was 4.230123 versus BF16's 5.528406; delta -1.298282
[-1.436853,-1.159142]. Comparing validation-selected checkpoints gives a smaller
delta -0.037204 [-0.047164,-0.027193]. The main test was not reused for this fix.

Full-precision projection/optimization is a reasonable numerical guard supported
by this matched failure case. It is not a multi-seed causal generalization claim.
The original archived runs and analysis identities remain unchanged. The new
FullPrecisionPhotonReference class disables outer autocast without changing the
architecture, parameter layout, objective or observation inputs.

-----------------------
An additional 12,000 records / 11,970 known families exclude every main/pilot
family. Folds are 9,594/1,221/1,185. Four methods receive equal observations,
seeds, epochs, batch size, optimizer and compute ceilings; actual FLOPs differ.

  Full temporal resolution, fresh     2.720802              3.952540

Removing the 256->64->256 temporal bottleneck helps on this fresh test, with the
same parameter count. Fine-minus-dense low-count delta is -0.342308
[-0.409386,-0.276561]. The residual head also improves the point estimate, less
than temporal resolution here. Fixed and fresh splitting are close at this budget.
These are finite exploratory mechanism comparisons, not a replacement 100k test
or a multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory search. GPU 1 auxiliary jobs can overlap;
their throughput is not an isolated architecture speed benchmark.

Registered p=.25/.75 shifts are complete without refitting. Low-count model/
selected-control NLLs are 3.881745/4.034534 at .25 and 4.252102/4.534081 at .75.
Stratum membership changes with p. Repeated views remain together under group
resampling and do not add independent events. Primary results are unchanged.

Analytic GT and response studies
-------------------------------
Models were trained on 6,000 exact analytic Poisson observations; 600 test records
represent 100 unseen source positions. Latent rates/positions stayed outside the
training loader. The oracle knows the latent source and has information unavailable
to a sparse-input model; a nonzero KL gap need not be removable from A alone.

The analytic result is negative against the selected simple control:
                                   All photons    Retained input count 1--10
  Learned models                      9.612301             10.406349
  Selected simple control             9.478690              9.955031
  Known-latent oracle                 7.832196              8.336437

Mean event KL(true expected location PDF || prediction): learned 1.706877,
control 1.575301. The analytic images show true expected PDFs, learned predictions,
controls and A/B arrival ticks. They do not use a P-ONE model as an in-domain toy
benchmark. Signal zero plus background one is not a zero-intensity state. A true
zero-intensity state has no conditional photon-location distribution.

Prescribed background, inactive support, timing and efficiency shifts, plus
merging, binned dead time and afterpulses, were evaluated without refitting.
The latter three violate the independent-Poisson observation assumptions. Their
scores are empirical predictive diagnostics, not physical calibration evidence.

Real calibrated pulse pilot
--------------------------
Train/validation/test are disjoint real runs 115150/121585/122252. Charge and
reported coordinates are preserved exactly. Because exact native DOM matching
was ambiguous across published geometries, this separately versioned experiment
uses 5,480 fixed spatial cells, 64 one-us bins and four charge-mark categories.
Each split unit is one whole pulse; fractional PE is never rounded into photons.

Selected real marked-pulse model: 3,972 parameters, epoch 2 selected on the
different validation run. Three epochs completed in 1,206.7 sec, 20.75 examples/sec,
1.783 GiB process peak and 0.379 GiB allocated GPU peak. The measured real test
took 141.2 sec, with 0.196 GiB allocated GPU and 1.544 GiB process peak.

The one reserved bright run gives joint location/time/charge-category NLL
9.932161 nats/pulse versus 13.849807 for the predeclared temporal mixture.
The model's arrival marginal NLL is 8.666843; it is a different score from the
joint marked score. Absolute scores must not be ranked across these datasets
because supports and target units differ.

This supports an empirical pulse-prediction result against the registered
temporal controls. Stronger geometry-aware real controls require a new frozen
comparison; this test must not be retuned. Live/DAQ exposure is unavailable,
reported clock tails are unresolved, positions are quantized and selections
change across runs. The fixed analysis window excludes 8,225/15,975 validation
pulses. Raw values remain stored. No clean-photon GT, exact Poisson, real low-count,
physical no-event or general new-run reconstruction claim follows.

Separate labelled utility probe
-------------------------------
Frozen main encoders are compared with raw A and the simple estimator using
equal-dimensional marginal/moment/count features on the fresh 9594/1221/1185
folds. Ridge fitting/scaling uses training labels; penalties use validation only.
Physics labels never enter self-supervised pretraining or photon-model inference.

Mean absolute log10-energy errors: raw 0.224396, baseline 0.221118, learned
0.215695. Learned-minus-baseline delta -0.005423 [-0.009346,-0.001877]. This is a
modest exploratory improvement on curated labelled simulation.

Mean angular errors: raw 84.427754 deg, baseline 84.399564 deg, learned
84.498839 deg. Learned-minus-baseline +0.099275 [-1.298229,+1.506175]: no clear
direction benefit. A limited linear probe does not establish end-to-end physics
utility, and these conditional group intervals do not include probe selection
or pretraining-seed uncertainty.

Verification and reproducibility
-------------------------------
Full test suite: 166 passed, eight warnings. Scoped lint checks passed. Actual
import, lazy/spawn epoch loading, count conservation, bounded donors, chunked
group intervals, exact optimizer resume, support/normalization, test locks,
GT diagnostics and marked-pulse integration were exercised.

A broad check also exposed a legacy checkpoint allowlist ordering assumption and
a nested/preexisting-entry bug. Scoped compatibility now adds only new entries;
regression checks preserve the caller's exact allowlist set. This does not alter
the photon-model study's computation.

All five main/mechanism/precision/continuation/pulse analysis identities match
the final frozen computation files and observations. The final evidence index
hashes all result reports. No original test result was overwritten, no revised
model was selected against the main test, and no external message was sent.
