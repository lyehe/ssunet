Longer photon-splitting pilot: execution and assessment
Date: 2026-10-25
Status: all three fixed-budget runs completed; larger independent study pending.

Finding
-------
The short pilot was substantially undertrained. Keeping the same architecture,
objective and learning rate, longer training reverses its low-count deficit
against the fixed and previously tuned retained-count/prior mixtures. The three
model seeds give very similar final scores. The all-event score still trails
the tuned mixture on its point estimate, and validation evidence is preliminary.

Fixed protocol
--------------
Configuration: config/neutrino_pilot.yml, written before training.
Runner: src/tools/neutrino_pilot.py.
Frozen metrics/controls: src/ssunet/neutrino_evaluation.py.

Same P-ONE water simulation shard, 60 sensors, 256 bins of 25 ns, fixed clock:
  1f927ea60a8327546c958f5bc5740d0f872ea0f38d1470271eb08f83958f2209
Same 800 training / 100 validation / 100 reserved test events as the short pilot.
Model: unchanged DensePhotonReference, 3,585 parameters, 16 channels.
Objective: mean raw-count -sum(B*log(q)) over a batch, without target-total
normalization. Inputs are A, fixed geometry/live support and p=0.5 only.
No clean photon ground truth, simulation labels or image PSNR are used.

Seeds: 42, 43, 44; 5,000 optimizer steps per seed; batch size 16.
AdamW learning rate 0.001 and gradient clipping at 1, matching the original.
BF16 training, FP32 probability normalization and frozen FP32 inference.
Each run processes 80,000 training examples: 100 passes over 800 distinct
events, with fresh event photon splits each epoch. These are not 80,000 new
physical configurations. Photon-split seed 42 is shared across model seeds;
initialization and training shuffle differ. Validation draws are identical.

Evaluation steps: 0, 110, 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 3000, 4000 and 5000.
Final step 5000 is the primary comparison. The best scheduled low-count
validation checkpoint is reported separately as a selection-based diagnostic.
All three best low-count checkpoints occur at step 4000.

Baselines and donor settings were frozen from the preceding pilot: no new
bandwidth or mixture search was performed. The short step-110 seed-42 model
and both stronger baseline scores reproduce within 1e-5 and 1e-8 respectively.
The test partition was not scored. No truth sidecar was read.

-------
Photon-weighted NLL, nats per held-out photon, lower is better.
All-event validation has 907 target photons in 55 nonempty-target events.
The 1--10 RETAINED-photon stratum has 50 events, 36 nonempty targets and 120
held-out photons. Its strata depend on A, not the full A+B count.

The means are means of seed-specific NLLs, not a probability-ensemble score.

before bootstrapping persisted configuration IDs together.

Final models: mean-seed model-minus-control differences, with descriptive
95% configuration-bootstrap intervals (10,000 replicates):
  Retained 1--10 vs fixed mixture:  -0.8480 [-1.4533, -0.3111]
  Retained 1--10 vs tuned mixture:  -0.5705 [-1.1926, -0.0100]
  All photons vs fixed mixture:    -0.0999 [-0.8036, +0.2028]
  All photons vs tuned mixture:    +0.2712 [-0.5048, +0.5949]
Negative differences favor the model. The low-count interval against the tuned
mixture only narrowly excludes zero. These are exploratory validation intervals,
conditional on the chosen models/baseline settings; they do not include model-
selection uncertainty or establish a confirmatory scientific result.

Event information and empty inputs
---------------------------------
Twenty donor realizations use training inputs matched on retained photon count,
with the same geometry, exposure and p. All retained 1--10 matches are exact.
Final donor-input NLLs at low counts are 8.719, 8.949 and 8.671 for seeds 42,
43 and 44, versus their own-input scores 4.115, 4.124 and 4.139. The mean model-
minus-donor difference is -4.654 [-5.493, -3.671], supporting event-specific
location/timing information beyond brightness. The all-event donor comparison
is not interpreted because the five brightest events lack exact count matches.

With zero retained photons, final mean NLL is 7.264, compared with 7.939 for
the tuned prior. That stratum contains only 15 target photons in 11 events.
It evaluates conditional photon-location prediction for empty inputs, not
physical no-event classification. Empty targets retain zero likelihood loss.

Learning curves
---------------
Most improvement occurs in the first 250--1000 updates. Low-count scores approach
a plateau at 2000--4000 and increase slightly from 4000 to 5000 in every seed.
Longer training resolves much of the original deficit without changing the
backbone. This experiment does not identify a need for a graph or sparse model,
nor prove that a different dense head is necessary. The remaining all-event gap
and the previously observed temporal-coarsening effect warrant later study.

Next step
---------
The low-count validation improvement now supports proceeding to a larger
controlled comparison before changing architecture. Keep the fixed mixture,
the frozen tuned estimator and exact count-matched controls. Increase the
independent validation/test sample and lock the training/selection protocol.
Current uncertainty is dominated by the small event sample, not seed variation.

For a 100,000-event cohort, persist source-level group partitions, preserve any
pilot development assignments that are reused, and exclude the entire pilot
cohort from the new test set. Review repeated source configurations and upstream
selection/clock/unit-photon provenance before claiming physical Poisson theory.
The current P-ONE source remains a curated simulation in water; this experiment
does not establish real-data or downstream neutrino-reconstruction benefit.

The frozen dense donor cache in this small-pilot runner should be replaced by
on-demand or bounded donor loading before a large-cohort run. Scaling should
measure CPU memory, import/setup cost and full evaluation cost as well as GPU
training throughput. The original 100,000-event independent-test study remains
pending; the three-seed experiment completed here uses the 1,000-event pilot.
