Completed all nine fresh fits: coarse temporal, full-time temporal and native
4D architectures, each with seeds 42/43/44 and 5000 optimizer updates. Only
study identity/output/scope metadata changed from the previous GAP protocol.
All source files were held fixed; 38 hash-verified training/native/reporting

Method and data
The original P-ONE training observations contain bins up to 444 photons.
These are photon counts and use GAP, following the user's acquisition policy.
True one-bit observations use the corrected Bit2Bit exponential-offset mask.
This GAP rerun does not benchmark the binary-specific correction, which does
not alter the GAP expression. The same 800 training / 100 validation events,
splits, geometry, 256 time bins, sine inputs, unit-mean targets and joint PMF
support are used. Reserved test records, clean photon GT and MC truth are
excluded. Held-out split photons are noisy observations, not clean targets.

Repeatability and integrity

(all photons / low count) compared with gap4d_retry_v1: 1.5805636e-6 nats/photon.
Both runs have identical validation event/configuration IDs and retained and
target photon counts. Per-event score differences and per-checkpoint floating
state differences are recorded in retry_comparison.json; weights are not
claimed bitwise identical. All nine fresh final checkpoint scores replay
with zero per-event NLL error. Probability mass error is at most 3.5763e-7,
invalid-support mass is zero, and data/source identities match the frozen run.
All 38 archived source files match their recorded SHA256 values. Earlier
study result files, checkpoints and identities remain preserved.
