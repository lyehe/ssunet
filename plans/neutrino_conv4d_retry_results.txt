4D convolution retry: completed 2026-10-27

Finding
The small full x/y/z/time model improves low-count held-out photon prediction
over both the old dense backbone and a full-time-resolution control in this
exploratory P-ONE simulation pilot. Its all-photon advantage over the full-time
control is uncertain. This supports trying spatial/time coupling for arrival
distributions; it is not proof of recovered real neutrino events or of a causal
advantage of self-splitting over alternative training objectives.

Efficiency and recovery
The 4D model has 3,945 parameters; each reference has 3,585. All use GPU 0.
4D training takes about 149-153 seconds per seed including loading/updates,
excluding evaluation/setup. Allocated GPU tensor peaks are 0.188-0.191 GiB;
recorded process peaks are at most 1.921 GiB. These are workstation timings
on a 60-sensor pilot, not a full-detector or isolated performance benchmark.

A daemon restart stopped seed 43 after its 1,000-update checkpoint. The seven
completed runs were preserved. Recovery restored model, optimizer, epoch and
batch cursor, then completed its original 5,000-step trajectory and seed 44.
A CPU interruption test reproduces uninterrupted weights and scores exactly.
The resumed timing includes the rounded pre-checkpoint time and new updates;
discarded uncheckpointed work and restart overhead are unmeasured/excluded.

Verification and limitations
All nine final checkpoints replay every saved validation event NLL exactly.
Training/model/native source hashes and the observed shard hash match their
frozen manifest; the completed pre-restart result hashes are unchanged.
Saved example probability distributions are normalized and nonnegative.
Native and portable 4D outputs and all parameter gradients agree on real
detector-grid inputs. Focused model/scoring checks and two recovery tests pass.

Figures show predicted distributions, retained A counts and noisy held-out B
counts on separate axes; B is not a clean photon GT image. Example records
were chosen by input-count stratum and record order, not prediction quality.
The source is curated P-ONE simulation with qualified generation/selection
assumptions. This reuses a small, previously explored validation cohort. The
intervals omit model selection, seed-fitting and unknown deeper-family
uncertainty. No new test, real-background detection or decoding claim is made.
