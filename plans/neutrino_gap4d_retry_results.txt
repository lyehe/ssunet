Corrected GAP 4D comparison, 2026-10-27

Method selection
The training shard has bins containing 444 photons. Its declared observation
kind is photon_counts, selecting GAP. Genuine one-bit acquisitions select
Bit2Bit. The loss rejects a conflicting method, nonbinary Bit2Bit data, and
overlapping binary splits. Count data allow A and B in the same bin.

Normalization
Raw-count ten-scale sine features follow the published GAP input convention.
Targets are normalized per observation inside the loss, following its unit-mean
target/log-mean-exp objective. The predicted distribution is one joint unit-sum
PMF over live sensor/time bins. Empty targets contribute zero loss; they are
excluded from event cross-entropy averages. Clean photon GT is unavailable
and unused. No GT intensity matching or PSNR is used.

Fixed experiment
800 training and 100 reused validation simulation observations; no test or
truth access. Three seeds per architecture, all 5000 updates, same p=.5 A/B
splits and FP32 optimization. Small detector backbones and the optimizer are
adaptations of the papers, not a microscopy benchmark reproduction.

Low means A has 1-10 retained photons: 50 validation observations, 36 with
nonempty targets, 120 held-out B photons. All-photon evaluation has 907 B
photons. Scores average seed-level event NLLs, not prediction ensembling.

4D minus full-time, whole-configuration descriptive bootstrap intervals:
  Low photon-weighted NLL: -0.4022 [-0.6740, -0.1594].
  All photon-weighted NLL: +0.1377 [-0.1737, +0.2873].
  Low equal-event CE:      -0.4699 [-0.8232, -0.1376].
  All equal-event CE:      -0.2889 [-0.5413, -0.0599].
The low-count advantage appears under both weightings. Across all photons,
4D is worse at the point estimate and the contrast remains uncertain. The
equal-event all-target comparison favors 4D. Changing the weighting changes
which observations dominate, so both views are retained.

Validation
14 normalization/model/resume cases and one reporting-statistic case pass;
scoped Ruff lint/format checks pass. All nine final checkpoints replay every
saved per-event validation NLL with maximum absolute error zero. Frozen
data, training sources and native-library sources match their identities.
Maximum checked output mass error is 3.5763e-7; invalid-support mass is zero.
4D training seconds per seed: 157.4, 177.5, 167.3. Peak allocated GPU memory
is 0.2089 GiB. Timings exclude validation/checkpoint overhead; GPU is shared.

Interpretation and limits
These are conditional descriptive intervals on a small, previously explored
simulation cohort. They exclude seed/selection uncertainty. No real-event
recovery, newly detectable observations, Poisson-physics validation, or causal
benefit of self splitting is established. Binary Bit2Bit data have not been
newly benchmarked here; their loss and gradient parity are unit checked.

The earlier raw-count-weighted/log1p run is preserved as conv4d_retry_v1.
Both encoding and training weighting changed; this is not an ablation of
target normalization alone. Its comparison remains valid for its objective.
