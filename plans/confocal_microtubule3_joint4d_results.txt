Microtubule 3: completed joint-channel 4D GAP trial
================================================

Completed 2026-10-28. Source: XYT cell microtuble 3.lif, Series003 (index 1),
both HyD 2 and HyD 4 detectors. The trial includes all 1024 time points and
all 64 Z planes in the central 256x256 XY crop. The NAS source was read-only.
This is a training and scored-patch reconstruction trial, not a completed
whole-acquisition reconstruction of the original 512x512 field.

The 17,974,018-parameter joint 4D model used widths 32/64/128/256, four
levels, ten photon features per detector, physical batch 2, accumulation 2,
16x16x128x128 TZYX patches, FP32 with TF32 disabled, and physical GPU 0.
The fixed 500-update endpoint completed in 3963.98 seconds (66.07 minutes).
Peak PyTorch-allocated memory was 18.788 GiB; reserved memory was 20.066 GiB.

Photon normalization and evaluation
----------------------------------
The source declares unaveraged 8-bit PhotonCounting. Values remain raw
integer counts; no detector-gain division, intensity normalization or
binarization was applied. This multi-count acquisition uses GAP.

One permanent binomial split assigns 80% of photons to training and 20% to
evaluation. Evaluation photons never enter any optimization input or target.
Training repeatedly splits its own pool 50:50. Each input therefore has
approximately 40% of the original count level. Both retained detector channels
provide joint input context. Each detector has its own GAP target weighting
and Barron log-softmax over TZYX. Neither detector serves as clean ground truth.

Twelve fixed patches cover early T=0, middle T=504 and late T=1008, with four
fixed ZYX locations in each phase. There are 278,826 reserved target photons:
84,661 in HyD 2 and 194,165 in HyD 4. The late patches contain only 214 of
these photons (73 and 141 by detector). The full raw cache contains 39,007,006
HyD 2 counts and 75,576,006 HyD 4 counts.

Final results: lower NLL is better
---------------------------------
All values are nats per reserved photon, with the same fixed inputs/targets.

  Method                   Pooled       HyD 2        HyD 4
  Joint 4D GAP              14.871011    14.264454    15.135485
  Fixed 4D Gaussian         15.105704    14.464577    15.385251
  Fixed 3D Gaussian         15.696176    14.890372    16.047527
  Uniform                   15.249238    15.249238    15.249238

The equal-detector mean NLL is 14.699969 for the network versus 14.924914 for
the 4D Gaussian control. The network improves on these particular fixed
Gaussian controls in both detectors. This is not a benchmark against all
traditional methods, tuned Gaussian controls or published neural checkpoints.
The Gaussian settings were fixed before fitting; no target-driven tuning.

  Phase       Target photons   Joint 4D GAP   4D Gaussian   Uniform
  Early       154,039          14.925738      15.106447     15.249238
  Middle      124,573          14.802692      15.097017     15.249238
  Late            214          15.246944      19.627468     15.249238

The early and middle data account for the predictive gain. The late neural
score is essentially at the uniform reference and has few photons. The 1%
uniform floor in the fixed Gaussian controls is especially poor in these
nearly dark cases. Their failure does not establish recovery of a hidden
biological signal by the network.

The learning curve was not monotonic: pooled NLL at steps 100/200/300/400/500
was 15.006471/14.918742/14.884146/15.016654/14.871011. The reported endpoint
remains the declared step 500. Intermediate checkpoints at 100,200,300,400
are retained for review. Only one model seed and acquisition were tested;
the patches are not independent biological replicates.

Visual assessment
------------------
The predeclared first spatial example in each phase is largely background.
Those examples are retained in the primary report. Supplemental figures show
every one of the twelve scored patches, avoiding selection of favorable views.
Time/depth means reveal broad cell structures in early/middle cases 2,3,6,7.
The network suppresses noisy texture but also strongly smooths structure,
including much of the detail in HyD 4. Fine microtubule recovery is not
established. Raw means are noisy contextual views, not clean references.

The raw count time course falls close to background around T=800--850 in
both detectors. Its cause is unknown; movement outside the sampled region,
illumination changes and photobleaching have not been distinguished. Do not
treat the late recording as labeled biological no-event data. There is no
clean-image PSNR or previously-undetectable-event claim from this trial.

Correctness and independent replay
---------------------------------
Twenty-two relevant CPU tests passed before fitting, including permanent
photon isolation, count conservation, joint-channel gradients, exact C=1
parity and exact interrupted/resumed optimizer trajectories. The three-update
full-size GPU readiness check passed before the 500-update run.

The independent final-checkpoint replay passed with exactly zero difference
from the stored final NLL. It verified raw/training-pool hashes, configuration,
all 12 local archived/current source files, the installed convolution sources
and checkpoint identity. An independent NumPy likelihood reduction verified
all 72 detector/case classical-control combinations; maximum difference was
5.69e-8 nats/photon.

Every exported case was checked for matching CTZYX coordinates, finite
nonnegative probabilities, unit mass and per-detector count calibration.
Maximum probability-mass error: 1.13565e-6 (FP32 normalization).
Maximum relative count-calibration error: 1.13559e-6.
Pointwise expected counts exactly equal q times that detector's observed total,
with the product rounded to float32. All nine historical training/inference
source hashes remain unchanged. Supplemental exports also reproduce each
scored case's reserved-photon likelihood.

Five real-data napari checks passed, covering both detectors, phase/patch
switching, physical Z orientation, movie/mean display and shared contrast.
Windows layer replacement now explicitly activates the viewer's OpenGL
context. The result viewer was launched successfully in 3D with all four
observed/predicted detector panels, initially on middle-phase case 6.
