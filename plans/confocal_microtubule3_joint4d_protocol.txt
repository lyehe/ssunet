Microtubule 3: fixed joint-channel 4D trial
========================================

Declared before full fitting on 2026-10-28.
Configuration: config/confocal_microtubule3_joint4d.yml
Output: data/confocal_microtubule3_joint4d_v1/

Source is XYT cell microtuble 3.lif, Series003 (index 1), both physical
detectors [HyD 2, HyD 4]. All 1024 times and all 64 Z planes enter the
central 256x256 XY crop, including the nearly dark final frames. Source
files remain read-only. Values are unaveraged integer photon counts;
recorded HyD gain is not a multiplier to undo in photon-counting mode.

Fit one joint 4D model, widths 32/64/128/256, depth four, 17,974,018
parameters, using 16x16x128x128 TZYX patches. Physical batch 2, gradient
accumulation 2, effective batch 4, 500 Adam updates, learning rate 1e-4,
gradient clipping 0.5, FP32, TF32 disabled, physical GPU 0 only. No early
stopping or checkpoint selection. Canonical example indices preserve the
same draws across equivalent microbatch grouping.

Photon isolation, without an acquisition holdout
-----------------------------------------------
One deterministic binomial thinning permanently assigns 80% of the
observed photons to the training pool. All remaining photons are reserved
for evaluation and never enter any optimization input or target, even if
the same coordinates are sampled again. Each training patch is split
50:50 within that training pool, independently per detector. Each network
input therefore has about 40% of the original acquisition's count level;
the training target has about 40% and the reserved evaluation target 20%.
There is no intensity rescaling, binarization, or assumed clean image.

All retained input detectors provide joint context. GAP supervision and
Barron log-softmax normalization are independent per detector over TZYX,
with equal detector weight in the objective. Outputs are conditional
photon-arrival distributions. Count maps are for display and multiply
each probability distribution by its own detector's observed patch total.
This calibration does not affect probability likelihood scoring.

Fixed evaluation
----------------
T starts: early 0, middle 504, late 1008 (16-time patches).
Local ZYX starts within the cache: (0,0,0), (16,0,128), (32,128,0),
(48,128,128). This gives four cases per phase, twelve total, both detectors.
Evaluation inputs are independently thinned halves of the training pool;
targets are always the permanent raw-minus-training complement.

Evaluate at steps 0,100,200,300,400,500. Record photon-weighted NLL in
nats/photon, equal-detector NLL, per-detector scores, phase-specific scores,
target photon totals, empty cases and probability sums. Lower NLL is better.
The final 500-update endpoint is primary. No PSNR against observed photons.

Controls use exactly the same retained input counts and reserved targets:
uniform; spatial Gaussian with sigma(T,Z,Y,X)=(0,0.5,1.5,1.5); and 4D Gaussian
with sigma=(1,0.5,1.5,1.5). Gaussian probabilities have a fixed 1% uniform
mixture, reflect boundary mode and 3-sigma support. No target-driven tuning.
The network has cross-detector context; these controls alone cannot isolate
the contribution of temporal convolution from architecture or detector context.

Predeclared visuals use the first spatial case in each phase, both channels.
Show a single time/Z slice and 16-time means, with a common display scale
per detector across phases and methods. Observations and temporal means are
noisy data. Late near-dark measurements have few target photons and do not
provide a biological no-event label or proof of previously undetectable signal.
Do not interpret these patches as independent biological replicates.

Verification and replay
-----------------------
The raw 8-GiB CTZYX cache is byte-checked against readlif at multiple times.
The separate 8-GiB training pool has a content hash, partition identity and
per-detector/per-time count totals. Checkpoints include optimizer, RNG,
step and complete local source/data/config identity. Sources are archived
before fitting. Training is locked to prevent duplicate workers.

Twenty-two relevant CPU tests passed before fitting, including registered
photon splits, independent detector normalization/loss gradients, exact C=1
parity and exact interrupted/resumed optimizer trajectory. Run the separate
three-update GPU readiness test, then the fixed full trial, then independently
replay the final checkpoint before reporting comparisons:

  .venv/Scripts/python.exe -m src.tools.confocal_joint_train --smoke-test
  .venv/Scripts/python.exe -m src.tools.confocal_joint_train --train
  .venv/Scripts/python.exe -m src.tools.confocal_joint_train --train --resume
  .venv/Scripts/python.exe -m src.tools.confocal_joint_report

Progress/checkpoints: training/progress.json, training/last.pt.
Results: training/result.json, comparison.json, index.html and comparison PNGs.
This study is separate from the previous HyD4-only acquisition-held-out trial;
its scores do not directly compare model quality across those different data
and photon-count conditions.
