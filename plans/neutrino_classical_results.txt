Traditional method comparison completed, 2026-10-28

Summary
Traditional methods are competitive on this pilot. Temporal Gaussian KDE has
the best all-photon point score, while the 4D neural predictor has substantially
better low-count photon prediction. A uniform advantage of the neural model
is not supported. This is the same explored simulation validation cohort.

Selection protocol
Configuration groups within the original 800 training observations were split
into 640 population-prior-fit and 160 inner-tuning observations, seed 20261028.
No inner-tuning event photons entered prior fitting during selection. All 614
predeclared prior/family/concentration combinations were evaluated; TV candidates
met the declared objective-gap convergence criterion. Selection minimizes mean
cross entropy over nonempty inner-training B targets. The lock was written and
hashed before reading outer validation photon arrays. The prior was then refitted
on all 800 training observations with its locked settings. No outer validation
data were used to choose parameters. Tuning took 14.62 seconds on this system.

Locked settings
Population prior: 100 ns temporal smoothing, equal-event weighting, .0001
pseudocount per live location.
Dirichlet shrinkage: concentration 3 observed photons.
Temporal KDE: 12.5 ns fixed bandwidth, no spatial smoothing, concentration 3.
Spatial/time KDE: 50 m spatial and 100 ns temporal base bandwidths, N_A^(-1/8)
scaling, concentration .1.
Poisson-TV: regularization 3, vertical strength 0 (temporal TV selected),
concentration 3. This is a Poisson-TV shape plus population-prior shrinkage.

The 1-10 retained-A subset contains 50 observations, 36 nonempty targets and
120 held-out photons. All-photon scoring contains 907 held-out photons across
100 observations and 55 nonempty targets. Five observations with A>30 supply
725 held-out photons, nearly 80% of the all-photon weighting.

4D minus temporal KDE, conditional whole-configuration bootstrap:
  Low-count photon NLL: -.743657 [-.958170, -.524445].
  All-photon NLL:       +.459082 [-.480029, +.985344].
Thus the low-count comparison favors 4D, while the pooled-photon KDE advantage
is a point estimate with an uncertain paired difference. Equal-event scores
favor 4D over every newly tested classical estimator as well. No global ranking
over all traditional algorithms, real data, or physical detection tasks is claimed.

Verification and resources
13 tests pass: mass conservation at kernel boundaries, dead support, physical
sensor permutation, analytic Dirichlet prediction, empty observations, prior
weighting, exact Poisson proximal/KKT equations, adjoint identities, analytic
two-bin TV solutions and disjoint training configuration groups. Scoped Ruff
lint/format checks pass. Numerical objective-gap tolerance for TV was tested
separately from parameter-error tolerance using exact small solutions.

The audit refits the locked prior and replays all five deterministic fields
for all 100 validation observations. Probability and event-NLL replay errors
are zero; maximum PMF sum error is 4.4409e-16 and invalid-support mass is zero.
The fit/tuning group separation, selection lock SHA, source/data/reference
identities and all 10 archived fitting/reporting source files are verified.
Earlier neural comparison files remain preserved.

Validation prediction times for all 100 observations: Dirichlet .0021 s,
temporal KDE .0041 s, spatial/time KDE .0065 s, Poisson-TV .2943 s. These are
setup-inclusive locked-estimator GPU measurements, not a matched comparison
with neural training time. Peak allocated GPU memory during the study was
.4917 GiB. The selected TV prediction converged in 450 iterations with maximum
primal-dual gap 9.6403e-5 per input photon, below the 1e-4 criterion.

Limits
The counts are curated P-ONE simulation, not real untriggered telescope data.
Poisson physics and latent source independence remain unverified. The validation
cohort was explored earlier; intervals condition on fitted estimators and omit
parameter-selection/model-seed uncertainty. The historical mixture had been
validation-tuned before this study and is labeled as a historical comparator.
Classical parameter choices came only from the declared training split.
Outputs predict relative photon locations, not event presence or absolute
arrival rates. No clean photon GT, PSNR, reserved test photons, MC truth or
target-derived inference intensity scaling was used.
