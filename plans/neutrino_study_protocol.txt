Prospective independent photon self-splitting study protocol
Frozen initial protocol: 2026-10-25
Configuration: config/neutrino_study.yml
Parent plan: plans/neutrino_phased_plan.txt

Objective and population
------------------------
Test low-count photon-arrival prediction using the unchanged dense model on a
new 100,000-event cohort of the locally acquired P-ONE simulation. Exclude the
entire pilot and all known exact-configuration relatives. Include catalogue
events with zero photon rows. Group by documented family identifiers where
available and otherwise by exact stored initial-state configurations, recording
that the archived table may not expose every latent family relation.

This is a qualified curated predictive-thinning study unless the source audit
establishes a stronger contract. Distinct event IDs, a photon table name and a
missing weight column do not certify physical conditional Poisson statistics.
The exact analytic generator is the reference for that statistical theorem.

No photon-count, target-count, nonemptiness or prediction-quality filter is used
to choose the cohort. Persistent source-group partition and selection hashes
are independent of photon detections. A nested 10k engineering cohort inherits
the same assignments. Test observations have their own file and are excluded
from all model/baseline-fitting and engineering-performance commands.

Fixed prediction and supervision
--------------------------------
Use all 60 fixed P-ONE water sensors and the original source time convention,
initially [0,6400) ns with 256 bins. A training-only clock/tail audit may require
a versioned correction before validation selection; no per-event target-derived
clock alignment or active-sensor selection is allowed. Retain live zero-hit
sensors and the original photon timestamps.

For p=0.5, draw A~Binomial(Y,p), B=Y-A from true integer observed counts. Preserve
multi-count/shared occupied bins, empty inputs and empty targets. Refresh
training splits each epoch using stable source event IDs. Freeze validation and
test splits independently of model initialization/shuffle. Train on mean over
events of -sum(B*log(q)); never divide each event by its realized target total.
The forward arguments are A, geometry/live support and p only. Physics truth
stays in evaluation-only files and never becomes a photon-learning target.

Model and compute
------------------
Use DensePhotonReference channels=16 (3,585 parameters), AdamW lr=0.001,
weight_decay=0.01, gradient clipping at 1 and BF16 updates with FP32 probability
normalization. Seeds are 42/43/44. Default batch 16 and ten complete passes,
approximately 50,000 updates/800,000 examples per seed for an 80k training fold.

Engineering profiling uses training observations only and at least 500 warm
updates. Any batch or whole-epoch budget correction needed to fit the measured
limits is recorded as a prospective configuration revision before the full
study's validation scores are used. The baseline search grids are fixed in the
configuration; do not weaken controls in response to their performance.

One explicitly selected RTX 4090: at most 1,800 update seconds per seed plus
600 evaluation/control seconds per seed, below 12 GiB peak tensor allocation
and a 4 GiB process-RSS target. Setup/import/baseline fitting are timed separately.
Budget exhaustion is reported as such, never as convergence. Read counts,
controls and bootstrap resamples in bounded batches.

Selection and primary analysis
------------------------------
Validate at epoch 0/1/2/5/final. Select each seed's checkpoint with the lowest
retained-input-count 1--10 validation photon NLL, breaking ties by earlier step.
Keep every seed and also report final-budget checkpoints. The primary test
score averages the three selected models' NLLs, not their probability ensemble.

Fit all prior counts on training observations. Choose the strongest simple
baseline by the SAME low-count validation metric from uniform, prior, fixed
50/50 mixture, frozen pilot 75/25 mixture and the prespecified smoothing/mixture
grid. Break ties by fixed candidate order and archive all candidate scores.
No baseline/test hyperparameter tuning follows test opening.

Generate 20 frozen training-donor views matched on retained counts, exposure,
geometry and p. Require exact count matches for interpreting the primary
conditioning control. Save donor IDs, not dense cohort-sized input arrays.

On untouched test events, in the predeclared 1--10 retained-photon stratum, define
  D = (sum mean_seed_NLL_model - sum NLL_control) / sum target_photons.
Compute paired 95% intervals using 10,000 whole-configuration bootstrap draws
in chunks of 64. Group any repeated realizations or p views together. Do not
resample photons or count model seeds as new test events. Report seed variation
separately from event-sampling uncertainty.

Support the restricted claim only if BOTH the model-minus-strongest-baseline
and model-minus-matched-donor upper interval endpoints are below zero, the point
improvement over the strongest baseline is at least 0.10 nats/photon, and the
resource/data checks pass. This practical margin is a study choice, not a
physical standard. Other baseline/stratum contrasts are secondary exploratory
results, with their multiplicity and source qualifications reported.

Publish all-event and retained-count 0/1--5/6--10/11--30/>30 scores, event and
photon denominators, empty-target rates, exact donor coverage, concentration by
event and full resource costs. Secondary p=0.25/0.75 views are distribution-shift
diagnostics of the p=0.5-trained model, not additional independent test samples.
An all-event disadvantage remains visible even if the low-count claim passes.

Test lock and continuation
--------------------------
Before scoring, seal a manifest of data/config/code/baseline/checkpoint hashes
and analysis settings. A separate test command requires that lock and refuses
to overwrite its first complete test record. Training and validation never
accept the test-shard path as a fitting source. A failed/inconclusive test is
preserved; revised models need a newly reserved independent test population.

Phase-6 ablations compare fine temporal output, prior/count residual heads,
fixed/fresh splits and response perturbations under matched budgets, when the
development evidence motivates them. Exact analytic rate diagnostics require
an analytic-data model, not unlabelled cross-domain P-ONE-to-toy comparison.
Phase 7 retains the real pulse/ADC detector contract and separates labelled
downstream probes from self-supervised pretraining. Empty triggered records are
not physical no-event labels. No image PSNR or clean-photon target is required.

Evidence freeze
---------------
Current reference artifacts are indexed in study/evidence_index.json with their
hashes. The 1k pilot, its three-seed 5k-step runs and the visualization bundle
remain exploratory development history. They are not retroactively registered
as independent confirmation of this protocol.
