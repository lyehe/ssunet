Completed confocal GAP comparison: effective batch four
======================================================

Completed 2026-10-28 on physical GPU 0 (RTX 4090). Both models finished the
registered 500-update schedule, each using 2,000 identical canonically indexed
training patches and fixed held-out photon splits. The primary endpoint is the
final update, with no early stopping or selection of an earlier checkpoint.

https://jonbarron.info/softmaxfix/
The teacher-logit cancellation correction is not applicable to these count
targets. Analytic FP32/FP64 numerical checks are in softmax_audit.json.

Verification
------------
53 relevant tests passed. Reloading both final checkpoints reproduced pooled,
equal-patch and every per-patch recorded NLL exactly. Maximum probability-mass
error was 8.34e-7 (4D) and 9.54e-7 (3D). The audit checked training source
snapshots, all 26 external convolution-package source files, cached counts,
source metadata, model configurations and validation photon draws.

Interpretation and limits
-------------------------
This is a modest likelihood improvement in one acquisition-holdout pilot.
There is one seed and one training budget. Independent biological replicates
have not been established; the 16 validation patches may overlap, so their
2,194,425 target-photon total is not a count of unique independent events.
No statistical significance or general superiority claim is established.

Visual inspection of the predeclared patches (0, 7, 15) shows spatial
structure, smoothing and visible row-wise banding in predictions, especially
4D. The cause of the banding is not established. A lower NLL alone does not
establish faithful recovery of fine structures or previously undetectable
observations. The temporal averages contain noisy A+B counts and may blur
motion; they are visual context, not clean ground truth. Predictions are
normalized photon-arrival distributions; PSNR is not used.
