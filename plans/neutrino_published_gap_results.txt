Published GAP transfer comparison completed, 2026-10-27 local time

Implementation and budget
The official GAP residual U-Net is vendored byte for byte at commit
135b8c67ce5a6ab4c44bcdd0fbe756f75526e249, with its MIT license. Network SHA256
3568742971dec5e83e5ebabfe30de1fb331c75bad47264fa439e58d8c6fca9ed.
The paper configuration has 6 levels, 28 initial channels, 10 sine scales and
33443425 parameters. A separate adapter maps raw detector counts to canonical
sensor/time rows, pads 60 rows to 64 for pooling, then crops before PMF scoring.

All models use the same 800 training and 100 validation P-ONE simulation
observations, photon splits, geometry and 256 arrival-time bins. No reserved
test records, MC truth, clean photon GT, intensity matching or PSNR is used.
The frozen 4D results are reused from gap4d_retry_v2 with all identities bound.

Three seeds for each predeclared GAP configuration:
  Paper setup: Adam 1e-4, clipping .5, batch 32, 2500 updates, validation-loss
    ReduceLROnPlateau factor .5 / patience 10, once per detector-data epoch.
  Matched setup: AdamW .001, weight decay .01, clipping 1, batch 16, 5000 updates.
Both process 80000 examples over 100 complete detector-data epochs, matching
the 4D reference. Both are reported at their fixed final update; no validation
selection of optimizer or checkpoint was used for the primary comparison.

Important training-duration diagnostic
The large U-Net degrades late in training on this small transfer dataset.
The best recorded mean low-count GAP matched curve point is 3.392283 at
48000 training examples, much closer to 4D's final 3.347322. The analogous
paper-setup value is 3.771901 at 16000 examples, versus its final 4.742118.
These minima were inspected retrospectively over saved curve summaries;
they are not selected-model scores, have no paired interval, and are not an
independent benchmark. Superiority over an early-stopped or fully tuned GAP
model remains unestablished. The viewer makes this qualification prominent.

Domain and training-schedule limits
This transfers the released architecture to detector sensor/time matrices;
it does not reproduce a microscopy benchmark. Whole-event matrices replace
256x256 random image crops; image flips/transposes are omitted and thinning
p=.5 is fixed. The original image notebook uses virtual epochs of 16000 random
crops, so its complete original image-training schedule is not reproduced.
Sensor-row adjacency differs from physical 4D adjacency, and no optimized
detector-specific GAP embedding or sensor-order sensitivity study is claimed.
This is not a Bit2Bit comparison, real-data validation, or detection proof.

Verification
Seven targeted tests pass: upstream-byte identity, full published capacity,
forward/parameter-gradient parity with released code, physical sensor mapping,
padding/dead-support behavior, source photon-loss parity, scheduler-safe
resumption and equal-event statistics (some properties share a test case).
Scoped Ruff checks pass. All six final published checkpoints replay every
saved validation-event NLL exactly. Maximum probability-sum error is 2.3842e-7
and invalid-support mass is zero. All current data/published/native/reference
identities and all 45 archived training/reporting source files are verified.
Earlier study results and checkpoints remain preserved.
