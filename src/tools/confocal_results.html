What the observed data look like

Three preselected validation patches. Raw input A and held-out B are noisy observations. The temporal mean is for visual context and may blur motion; it is not ground truth.

Predicted arrival distributions

Both models receive the same A photons. Predictions are probabilities on the full TZYX patch; these central slices use one common probability color scale per patch. Visible smoothing and row-wise banding need further characterization; lower NLL alone does not establish faithful recovery of fine detail.

Fixed setup

This pilot holds out a separate acquisition. Independent biological replicates have not been established, and validation patches can overlap. One seed and one fixed training budget cannot establish general superiority or discovery of previously undetectable structures.
